MBA Roadmap — required classes by semester

Texas Full-Time MBA structure per the published curriculum — core is front-loaded in year one, year two is electives plus the two program requirements. Confirm hour totals and custom-core menus with your academic advisor.

Required core
8 classes · ≈16 hrs
7 in fall plus Strategic Management in spring — core classes run 2 hrs each (course numbers 28x)
Custom core
4 classes · 8 hrs
2 hrs each, chosen from the core menu: #1 in fall, #2–#4 in spring
Electives
≈12 classes · ≈36 hrs
Typically 3 hrs each (38x/39x numbers); the Worldview and R&I requirement courses count within these
Concentration — Global Business
within electives
12 hrs incl. one I B course (Option A) · 9 hrs + STEP (Option B) · 6 hrs + semester abroad (Option C)

Class counts assume the commonly cited 60-hour full-time MBA; the public curriculum page doesn't publish per-type hour totals, so treat the ≈ figures as planning estimates and confirm the exact split with your advisor.

Global Business Concentration

From the Texas Full-Time MBA concentration sheet (2024–25) — fulfill one of the three options below. Rules may have shifted since that sheet; confirm with your academic advisor.

Option A
12 credit hours of approved courses, at least one carrying the I B prefix
Option B
9 credit hours plus one Short-Term Exchange Program (STEP)
Option C
6 credit hours plus one long semester abroad (3+ UT courses while abroad)

Approved courses — and what the Spring 2027 schedule offers

  • I B — any graduate course — offered: the five Global Connections trips (Portugal, New Zealand, Italy, S Korea, Mexico), Busn/Geopolitics/Diplomacy, Global Net Zero Strategy, and the web-based Studies in Global Mgmt
  • BGS 380E Global Political Economy — offered, MW 9:30–11:00a (Martinez Espinosa)
  • MAN — Global Management — offered as MAN 385.51, T 3:30–6:30p (Doggett)
  • BGS 380 Global Regulatory Policy — not on the Spring 2027 schedule
  • MAN 385 Management & Marketing in the Global Arena — not on the Spring 2027 schedule
  • MKT 382 Invisible Global Marketing — not on the Spring 2027 schedule
  • RM 395 / PA 388K Managing International Risk — not on the Spring 2027 schedule
  • UT Foreign Language — any UT foreign-language course counts (not listed on this business schedule)

Courses marked GB in the table count toward the concentration; STEP rows satisfy Option B's program leg.
